QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A clearer way to
get started.

Is BSLDesk an interpreter agency?

No. BSLDesk is planned as software for agencies and their clients. Agencies remain responsible for sourcing and paying interpreters under their own arrangements.

Can an NHS practice use this now?

This is a demonstration and discovery-stage product. It is not approved or deployed for NHS patient information. A real NHS pilot would need organisation-specific procurement, security, data protection and applicable digital assurance checks.

Does an interpreter have to pay?

The current business concept is free participation for interpreters, with the agency paying for the software subscription. Interpreter work is paid separately.

Can we use real booking information in this demo?

No. This public demonstration intentionally contains fictional requests, has no database, and is not suitable for patient information or confidential bookings.

Will there be an iPhone or Android app?

Potentially later. The first goal is a responsive web application that works on phones and computers. Native mobile apps are not part of the initial pilot.
